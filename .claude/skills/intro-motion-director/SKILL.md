# Intro & Motion Director

Create one memorable orchestrated motion moment instead of animating everything.

## Principles
- Motion communicates physical mass, hierarchy or transition.
- Prefer transforms and opacity.
- Heavy objects accelerate and settle with weight.
- Glass uses restrained reflection shifts.
- Mechanical parts move on plausible rails/joints.
- Light sweeps follow the material and established light direction.
- Sparks/electricity require a visible cause.
- Keep primary controls stable while users interact.

## Intro pattern
1. environment/foundation appears
2. hero physical object establishes
3. controlled material/light reveal
4. content and CTA become usable
5. ambient motion falls into a quiet idle state

Never make the user wait on decorative animation.

## Performance
- prefers-reduced-motion: immediate/simplified reveal
- LOW: CSS/static
- MEDIUM: normal motion, reduced particles
- HIGH: full effects
- stop hidden/offscreen loops
